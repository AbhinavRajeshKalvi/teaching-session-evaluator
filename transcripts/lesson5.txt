Teacher: Let's discuss why recycling is important.

Student: It reduces waste.

Teacher: Exactly. Can you think of another reason?

Student: It helps protect the environment.

Teacher: Great! What happens if we don't recycle?

Student: More garbage goes into landfills.

Teacher: Right. Can recycling also save energy?

Student: Yes, because making new products often requires more energy.

Teacher: Excellent point.

Student: I think recycling also reduces pollution.

Teacher: That's absolutely correct.
