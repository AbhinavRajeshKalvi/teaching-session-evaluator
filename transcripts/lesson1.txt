Teacher: Good morning! Today we'll learn about fractions.

Student: I've always been confused about fractions.

Teacher: That's okay. Imagine a pizza cut into two equal slices. If you eat one slice, you've eaten one-half of the pizza.

Student: Oh, so one-half means one out of two equal parts?

Teacher: Exactly! Now imagine the pizza is cut into four equal slices. If you eat one slice, what fraction have you eaten?

Student: One-fourth.

Teacher: Great! Which is larger, one-half or one-fourth?

Student: One-half because the slices are bigger.

Teacher: Excellent reasoning! You understood that very quickly.

Student: That actually makes a lot more sense now.
