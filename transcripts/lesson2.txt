Teacher: Today we're solving linear equations.

Teacher: Solve x + 5 = 12.

Student: I don't know where to start.

Teacher: We need to isolate x by subtracting 5 from both sides.

Student: Why both sides?

Teacher: Because we must keep the equation balanced.

Student: I'm still confused.

Teacher: Imagine a weighing scale. Whatever you do on one side, you must also do on the other.

Student: Oh, that makes more sense.

Teacher: Great. Now what's 12 minus 5?

Student: Seven.

Teacher: So x equals?

Student: Seven.

Teacher: Correct!
